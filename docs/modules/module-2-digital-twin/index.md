# Module 2: Digital Twin Simulation (Gazebo & Unity)

Welcome to the Digital Twin Simulation module! This module focuses on physics-based simulation and environment modeling for humanoid robots, designed specifically for AI students with basic robotics concepts.

## Overview

In this module, you'll explore the fascinating world of digital twin technology applied to robotics. You'll learn how to create virtual replicas of physical robotic systems using industry-standard simulation tools.

Digital twins are virtual representations of physical systems that enable testing, validation, and optimization without the need for expensive hardware. In robotics, this approach allows students and researchers to experiment with robot behaviors, sensor configurations, and environmental interactions in a safe, cost-effective environment.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, you will be able to:
- Understand and configure physics simulations using Gazebo
- Create high-fidelity environments in Unity for robot interaction
- Simulate various sensors including LiDAR, depth cameras, and IMUs
- Apply digital twin concepts to humanoid robot development

## Prerequisites

Before starting this module, you should have:
- Basic understanding of robotics concepts
- Familiarity with fundamental physics principles (gravity, collisions, dynamics)
- Understanding of sensor systems in robotics

## Module Structure

This module is organized into three main chapters:

1. **Physics Simulation with Gazebo** - Learn about gravity, collisions, and dynamics
2. **High-Fidelity Environments in Unity** - Explore rendering and human-robot interaction
3. **Sensor Simulation** - Understand LiDAR, depth cameras, and IMUs

Each chapter builds upon the previous one, but can also be studied independently depending on your specific interests.

## Getting Started

Begin with Chapter 1: Physics Simulation with Gazebo to establish the foundation for digital twin simulations. If you're already familiar with physics concepts, you may jump to any chapter that matches your current knowledge level.
